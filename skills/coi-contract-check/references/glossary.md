# COI contract check glossary

Load this file when a term in the documents is unclear.

These entries are general documentary labels to help read what a page is, not advice about what a policy covers or a statement of any policy’s terms.

- **Certificate of insurance (COI):** a document (often ACORD 25) that summarizes coverages and limits; it is not the policy itself.
- **Additional insured:** a party added to a policy so that the policy may respond to claims against that party arising from the named insured’s work.
- **Waiver of subrogation:** wording that prevents the insurer from seeking recovery from another party after paying a claim.
- **Primary and non-contributory:** wording stating that the policy responds first and does not seek contribution from other insurance.
